Common questions

Why does WearMore ask me to sign in with Apple?
It's just a front door for the app - WearMore has no account system or server of its own. Your closet data lives in your personal iCloud account regardless of this screen.

How do I get my closet on another device?
Sign in to the same Apple ID on your other device and make sure iCloud is turned on. Your folders, items, looks, and packing lists sync automatically.

Why didn't the background get removed from my photo?
Background removal runs on-device and needs a real iPhone or iPad - it isn't available in a simulator. If it misses a spot, use the erase/restore brush to touch it up by hand.

How do I delete something?
Delete any folder, item, look, or packing list from within the app. "Sign Out" in Settings only signs out of the Apple ID gate - it does not delete your closet data.
