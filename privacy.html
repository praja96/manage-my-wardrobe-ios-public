Privacy Policy · Last updated October 26, 2026

What we collect

WearMore has no servers of our own and no account system. Signing in with Apple is only a front door for the app - we request no name, email, or contact information, just an opaque identifier that's stored locally on your device to keep you signed in. We never see it and nothing is sent to us.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your name, email, location, contacts, browsing history, or an advertising identifier. This app has no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no backend server - the only systems it ever talks to are Apple's own (Sign in with Apple, iCloud/CloudKit, on-device Vision processing).

Where your data lives

Your closet photos, items, outfits ("Looks"), calendar entries, insights, and packing lists are stored in your own iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit, and on your device. They sync automatically between your devices signed into the same Apple ID, exactly like iCloud Photos or Notes. We have no access to this data, cannot read it, and cannot recover it for you if it's lost - Apple's iCloud privacy terms govern how it's stored and encrypted.

On-device processing

Removing the background from a clothing photo happens entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Photos are never uploaded anywhere to do this.

Deleting your data

Delete any folder, item, look, or packing list directly in the app at any time. To remove everything, delete the app from your device and, if you want it removed from iCloud too, turn off WearMore's iCloud sync (or delete its data) from your device's Settings > Apple ID > iCloud > Manage Account Storage. "Sign Out" in Settings only clears the local Apple ID gate and does not delete any closet data.
